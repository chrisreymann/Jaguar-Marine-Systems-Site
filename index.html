01 / System concept

Six mission cells.
A dedicated ROV dock.

Each cell has a defined job. The first four guide, sort, collect, and drain the biomass. A nursery cell provides space for habitat work, while a service cell and an underwater drone dock support operations and inspection.

Proposed handling sequence

From intake to bag transfer

  1. C1Guide floating sargassum to intake
  2. C2Sort material and allow wildlife passage
  3. C3Collect biomass and feed the conveyor
  4. C4Inspect and drain biomass in bags
  5. C6Transfer filled bags for pickup

The proposed layout uses nets between selected cells, guarded conveyors to move biomass, and dewatering bags for drainage and pickup. Wildlife passage and bag handling are planned test subjects.

C1 Intake

Living buffer and intake

Guide booms would steer floating sargassum into a sheltered approach. A permeable buffer and flow corridor are intended to guide the biomass toward sorting.

What to test How much biomass enters the corridor under changing currents, and where it accumulates.

C2 Sort

Ecological sorting

Between-cell nets and screens would guide sargassum toward collection. The layout reserves bypass paths for wildlife and access for clearing debris.

What to test Wildlife passage, debris buildup, and whether the screens remain accessible for cleaning.

C3 Collect

Collection and transfer

A collection bay would regulate the feed onto a guarded conveyor. The conveyor would move wet biomass from the waterline to the dewatering cell.

What to test Feed consistency, wet-biomass throughput, and access for clearing jams.

C4 Drain

Dewatering and inspection

Operators would inspect the collected material before loading it into permeable bags. The bags would drain before handling and transfer.

What to test Drainage time, filled-bag handling, and how to collect or manage the drainage water.

C5 Nursery

Nursery and restoration

Removable habitat cartridges would give the team a way to exchange or inspect nursery substrates from the hexagonal frame. The cartridge has been modeled as one part with integrated lifting eyes.

What to test Cartridge access and retrieval, along with habitat performance in future trials.

C6 Service

Service and logistics

A service deck would provide a place to stage filled bags, handle equipment, and transfer loads to a workboat. It would also give the crew access to maintain the linked cells.

What to test Deck clearance, bag transfer and lifting workflow, and workboat approach.

C7 Monitor

Underwater drone dock

The dock would give a tethered remotely operated vehicle (ROV) a defined place to return after inspection or sampling. The monitoring concept also includes aerial surveys of the array and surrounding water.

What to test Docking approach, clearance, tether routing, and recovery in a controlled setup.

Next milestone

Engineering validation and a one-cell prototype

We’re seeking support to review the structural model, define a prototype test plan, and fabricate one cell for bench testing.

  • 01
    Structural review. Check load paths, support reactions, and assembly interfaces.
  • 02
    Fabrication and bench tests. Check assembly fit, access for servicing, and handling procedures.
  • 03
    Water trial planning. Define what to measure for biomass handling, wildlife passage, and monitoring before a controlled trial.
